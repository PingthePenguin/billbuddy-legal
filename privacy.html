Information we process

BillBuddy may process your account email and Firebase user identifier, bill names, amounts, monthly due days, paid status, SMS consent status, and the phone number configured to receive SMS reminders. The Android app stores bill details on your device and synchronizes them to Firebase when you sign in. Our server configuration may store the recipient phone number separately from your bill records.

How we use information

We use these details to show the bill dashboard, synchronize your bills, identify upcoming payments, and send reminders if you enable SMS. We use Google Firebase for authentication, storage, and server execution and Twilio for SMS delivery. These service providers process relevant information to provide the service.

SMS opt-in and data sharing

SMS enrollment is optional and can be disabled in the app. You may also reply STOP to an SMS to stop receiving texts. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
