About BillBuddy

BillBuddy helps you record bills and track due dates. It is an informational reminder tool and does not pay bills or guarantee that payments will be on time. You remain responsible for checking due dates and making payments.

SMS Terms

By checking the optional SMS consent box in the BillBuddy application and saving your preference, you consent to receive recurring automated bill reminder text messages at your configured mobile number. Messages identify bills and upcoming due dates; they are not marketing messages. Reminder messages may be sent 7, 3, and 1 days before an unpaid bill is due and on the due date. Message frequency varies based on the number of bills you enter, with up to four scheduled reminders per unpaid bill per month, excluding any test or administrative texts.

Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not required to use the rest of BillBuddy. Reply STOP to unsubscribe from SMS messages or HELP for assistance. You may also turn off SMS reminders in the application. Message delivery depends on external providers and is not guaranteed.

Acceptable use and accuracy

You are responsible for keeping bill names, amounts, and due dates accurate, maintaining access to your account, and providing a phone number that you own or are authorized to use.
